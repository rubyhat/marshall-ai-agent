# Collect and Grade Reference Evidence

Build a small, traceable evidence set and preserve the difference between fact and interpretation.

## Prefer sources in this order

1. Current direct observation of the official product.
2. Current official documentation, help center, release notes, or product materials.
3. User-provided screenshot or recording with known context.
4. Reliable current secondary material.
5. Search snippets, historical captures, or unattributed examples as weak signals only.

Use multiple sources when one source cannot establish the behavior or freshness. Do not collect more sources after the question is sufficiently answered.

## Record provenance

For each decisive source, capture applicable:

- title, product, and direct URL or artifact path;
- access or observation date;
- direct, official, user-provided, secondary, or historical source type;
- locale, country, viewport, device, platform, authentication, and plan;
- accessible scope and missing states;
- whether the evidence is live, cached, archived, or inferred;
- confidence and known contradictions.

## Label every material claim

- `observed`: directly visible in the inspected product or artifact;
- `documented`: stated by an official source;
- `inferred`: reasoned from evidence but not directly confirmed;
- `unknown`: evidence is insufficient or contradictory.

Do not describe a search snippet as live behavior or a secondary screenshot as the current complete layout.

## Handle blocked access

- Do not bypass login, paywall, CAPTCHA, anti-bot, geography, or other access controls.
- Try permitted official documentation or user-provided evidence.
- State which states, routes, devices, or actions remain unobserved.
- Lower confidence instead of filling gaps with assumptions.

## Store evidence selectively

Persist a screenshot or capture only when it is permitted, decisive, reusable, and explicitly within artifact authority. Prefer a cropped or minimal capture that avoids personal data and unrelated content.

Do not store full-site archives, copied marketing libraries, user data, credentials, or copyrighted assets for reuse in the target product. Link to the source when a local copy adds little value.
